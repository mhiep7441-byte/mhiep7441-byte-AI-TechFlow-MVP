Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026

This policy explains how AI TechFlow Studio handles information when users create videos and connect TikTok.

1. Information processed

The application may process task details, research sources, scripts, captions, media files, TikTok basic profile information, OAuth tokens, privacy choices, and submission status.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to create requested content, identify the connected account, submit user-approved videos, display processing status, and maintain security.

3. TikTok data

Basic TikTok profile data helps users verify the connected account. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and used only for actions explicitly authorized by the user. AI TechFlow Studio does not sell TikTok data.

4. Storage and security

Application data is stored in configured service environments. Secrets are excluded from source control. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no system is completely secure.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with providers needed to perform requested actions, including OpenAI, Cloudinary, and TikTok, subject to their policies. Data is not sold for advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

Users may delete tasks and generated files and revoke TikTok access. Revocation stops future access. Repository contact may be used for hosted-data deletion requests.

7. Children

The service is not directed to children under the minimum age required by TikTok or applicable law.

8. Contact

Privacy requests may be submitted through the project repository.
