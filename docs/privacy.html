Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026

This policy explains how AI TechFlow Studio handles information when users create videos and connect a TikTok account.

1. Information processed

The application may process task titles, video topics, generated scripts, captions, media files, TikTok basic profile information, OAuth tokens, and upload status.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to create requested content, identify the connected TikTok account, upload user-approved drafts, display processing status, and maintain application security.

3. TikTok data

Basic TikTok profile data is shown so users can verify the connected account. OAuth tokens are used only for actions authorized by the user. AI TechFlow Studio does not sell TikTok data.

4. Storage and security

Application data is stored locally or in the configured service environment. Secrets are excluded from source control. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no system is completely secure.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with service providers necessary to perform requested actions, including OpenAI and TikTok, subject to their respective policies. Data is not sold for advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

Users may delete local tasks and generated files and may revoke TikTok access. Revocation stops future access. Repository contact may be used for deletion requests relating to hosted service data.

7. Children

The service is not directed to children under the minimum age required by TikTok or applicable law.

8. Contact

Privacy requests may be submitted through the project repository at GitHub.
